What I do

Fifteen years of backend engineering, most of it in environments where correctness, latency and auditability are non-negotiable: core banking, post-trade settlement, CFD trading, real-time sports data and high-traffic publishing.

Event-driven & distributed systems

Designing and operating Kafka-based pipelines, CQRS and event-sourced services, and the delivery guarantees, idempotency and replay semantics that make them trustworthy in production.

Domain-driven design in finance

Modelling settlement, order management and risk domains so the code speaks the business language — boundaries that survive regulation changes and team growth.

High-throughput, low-latency services

Market-data ingestion, pricing and real-time feeds where throughput is measured in the thousands of events per second and a slow consumer is an incident.

Legacy modernisation

Incrementally carving services out of monoliths without stopping delivery — strangler migrations, contract-first APIs and test harnesses around code nobody dares touch.
